The rule everything follows from

Six kinds of statement. None of them looks like another.

Fact

Measured. Carries a source or it is not one.

Source claim

What a publication asserts. Attributed to it.

Human hypothesis

A person's proposal. Argued, not asserted.

AI hypothesis

A machine's proposal. Marked as such, always.

Inference

Derived. The derivation is shown.

Unknown

Admitted. The product says so out loud.

Hypotheses

An object you
can attack.

Claim, mechanism, required assumptions, unknowns, risks — declared up front. Supporting and contradicting evidence sit side by side.

Attacking an idea well is what earns standing here.

The agents

Eleven narrow agents. Not one omnipotent one.

Scout

Researcher

Synthesizer

Scientist

Engineer

Economist

Skeptic
Finds the weaknesses
Red team
Tries to break the hypothesis
Simulator
Runs the models that exist
Editor
Clarity without changing meaning
Curator
Eligibility only. Never publication
